Identity and scope notes:

- 3ZR-FAE remains distinct from the existing 3ZR-FE: the manufacturer tables give separate code rows, grades and ratings.
- 2AZ-FXE and LEB store combustion-engine output and torque only. Toyota's 140 kW hybrid-system output, Honda's 101 kW system output, and all electric-motor figures are excluded.
- C30A and C32B share one NSX table but remain separate identities and transmission/application columns. The same column discipline is applied to J25A/J32A, F23A/J30A, and the three Fit engines.
- The H22A ranges are envelopes of two discrete documented versions: SiR 4AT and S spec 5MT. They do not describe a continuous rating or every H22A.
- Recurring faults, oil service fills, block material and service-life estimates are not supplied by the selected sources and remain unclaimed.
- Honda Civic B16A, D13B and D15B, and Accord F18B/F20B were checked during candidate selection. The older archive tables available in this pass express performance in PS/kg-m; they were not needed because 15 other unique codes had direct official kW/N-m tables. They are left for a future batch rather than introducing derived values here.
- The Toyota Harrier history page was useful for a 1MZ-FE application lead but did not provide the complete performance pair. The Alphard G/V launch table supplies code, power and torque for the stored 1MZ-FE specification.
