Identity and scope notes:

- Chevrolet Performance product names are treated as the exact identities: each accepted row has its own marketed designation and, except where the current ZZ427/480 page prints an ambiguous part-number string, its own published part number. No generic LS, Small-Block or Big-Block family ranges are created.
- `LS364/450 Long Block`, `LS9 Long Block` and `LSX376-B15 Long Block` are explicitly limited to the documented U.S. long-block product versions. Their external induction or boost configuration is not generalized to related production engines.
- The 350, 383, 427, 454, 502, 572 and 632 records remain separate only where Chevrolet publishes a distinct product name and performance pair. Equal figures on HT383 and HT383E do not merge the products because the manufacturer supplies separate names and part numbers.
- Every Mercedes-Benz suffix record uses an official model archive that supplies engine type, model designation, production boundary, construction, power, torque and rpm. `M156` uses the official C-Class Estate series page for the AMG 6.3-litre V8 performance pair and a second official image record for the exact M156 code, 6,208 cc displacement and manifold-injection mapping.
- `M104.990` and `M104.994` are separate exact engine types on the same S 320 archive page and are split at the stated June 1993 change. The M119.970, M119.971 and M120.980 ranges contain only the two factory calibrations explicitly bounded before and from September 1992.
- Recurring faults, service-life estimates and oil service fills are not supplied by the selected specification pages and remain unclaimed.

- 3ZR-FAE remains distinct from the existing 3ZR-FE: the manufacturer tables give separate code rows, grades and ratings.
- 2AZ-FXE and LEB store combustion-engine output and torque only. Toyota's 140 kW hybrid-system output, Honda's 101 kW system output, and all electric-motor figures are excluded.
- C30A and C32B share one NSX table but remain separate identities and transmission/application columns. The same column discipline is applied to J25A/J32A, F23A/J30A, and the three Fit engines.
- The H22A ranges are envelopes of two discrete documented versions: SiR 4AT and S spec 5MT. They do not describe a continuous rating or every H22A.
- Recurring faults, oil service fills, block material and service-life estimates are not supplied by the selected sources and remain unclaimed.
- Honda Civic B16A, D13B and D15B, and Accord F18B/F20B were checked during candidate selection. The older archive tables available in this pass express performance in PS/kg-m; they were not needed because 15 other unique codes had direct official kW/N-m tables. They are left for a future batch rather than introducing derived values here.
- The Toyota Harrier history page was useful for a 1MZ-FE application lead but did not provide the complete performance pair. The Alphard G/V launch table supplies code, power and torque for the stored 1MZ-FE specification.
